Privacy Policy

COSTRON – Project Costing, Optimisation, Scheduling & Tracking · Last updated 28 October 2026

COSTRON is a web application that runs entirely in your browser. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

1. What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. COSTRON has no user accounts, no database and no server of its own. The website is static files hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement.

1a. Sign-in accounts

If this site uses the COSTRON sign-in page, your email address and password are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. The password is stored only in hashed form by Firebase and is never visible to us. Your email address is used only to sign you in and to send password reset or confirmation emails that you request. You can ask for your account to be deleted at any time using the contact details below.

2. Your project data

Projects you create are stored in your own browser (local storage) on your device and, if you choose, in files you save to your computer or to your own cloud storage. They are never sent to us.

3. Cloud storage you connect

If you sign in to Google Drive, Dropbox, Microsoft OneDrive or a Nextcloud/WebDAV server, COSTRON connects from your browser directly to that service, using its official sign-in. COSTRON never sees your password. The access granted is used only to list, open, create and save the COSTRON files you choose. Sign-in tokens are kept only in your browser, and you can remove them at any time with Sign out in COSTRON or in your account's security settings.

4. Google user data

With Google Drive, COSTRON requests only the drive.file permission. This gives it access only to files that COSTRON creates, or that you select with the Google file picker. The data is used solely to open and save those files for you, in your browser. It is not transferred to us or to third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. COSTRON's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Cookies and tracking

COSTRON uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage only for your projects and your settings.

6. Third-party code

For PDF and Excel export, COSTRON loads open-source libraries (jsPDF, JSZip) from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. It loads the sign-in libraries of Google or Microsoft only when you choose to connect those services.

7. Deleting your data

To delete projects from your device, clear this site's data in your browser settings. To delete files in your cloud storage, delete them there. To revoke access, sign out in COSTRON and remove "COSTRON" from your Google, Dropbox or Microsoft account's connected apps.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. For questions, contact kuenchapdhendup.btn@gmail.com.
